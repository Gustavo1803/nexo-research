# Content and asset notes

## Research-area pages and recent developments

Four area pages are available in English and Spanish: transportation and mobility; supply chains and resilience; digital markets and economics; food systems and trade. They explain the agenda and connect dated global developments to related work from the shared research and project catalogs. The header uses a larger NEXO logo and the group name in its upper-right line.

Global developments were reviewed on **October 2, 2026**. Every item links directly to its primary source and displays its publication date or verified publication month. Summaries are original paraphrases. The separately labelled “Connection to our research” is an interpretation for this site; it does not imply that Gustavo authored, endorsed, or contributed to the source report. Related working papers and work in progress retain the status in the research catalog.

Sources include [UNCTAD’s September WTO forum briefing](https://unctad.org/news/unctad-calls-stronger-cooperation-build-resilient-inclusive-trade-wto-forum), [World Bank support for Madagascar’s transport network](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/04/28/madagascar-s-transport-infrastructure-gets-a-major-boost-with-world-bank-group-support), [WTO’s World Trade Report 2026 launch](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/wtr_15sep26_483_e.htm), [World Bank Trade Watch](https://www.worldbank.org/en/brief/2026/04/21/trade-watch), [World Development Report 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/wdr2026), [OECD’s July AI competition paper](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/competition-in-the-age-of-ai_6f88a1ea-en.html), [FAO’s October food-price release](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/fao-food-price-index-rises-in-september-amid-weather-concerns-and-transport-disruptions/en), and [FAO’s July agricultural-market report launch](https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/state-of-agricultural-commodity-markets-explores-how-best-to-cope-with-shocks/en).
